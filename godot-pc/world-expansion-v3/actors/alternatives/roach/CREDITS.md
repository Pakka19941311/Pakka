## Adaptation by the VARENDOR project, 2026-09-12

Converted legacy Blender material to Principled / GLTF, retained source diffuse and tangent normal textures (normal strength0.5, roughness0.7), selected original idle, attack2 and death actions, preserved the original NLA Walk and Flee windows/timing, added a clearly authored local spine/head recoil named hit, normalized the idle height to0.45m and foot-plane origin, rotated forward, baked source constraints and a sampled upward floor correction. Source geometry and UV layout were retained. Source specular map is not used as an invented metalness map. Maximum texture dimension1024px.
