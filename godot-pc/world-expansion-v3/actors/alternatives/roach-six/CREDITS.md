# Six-leg Roach adaptation for VARENDOR

Source: [Roach, game ready and animated](https://opengameart.org/content/roach-game-ready-and-animated), original model by Atmostatic; rigging, animation and retexture by Danimal. License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Full legal text is included alongside this file.

This builds on the separately preserved V3RoachCandidate.glb adaptation. That basis normalized scale/orientation/floor, retained source clips and NLA walk/flee windows, and added local hit recoil. New changes: two actual skinned middle legs with root, upper, lower, claw joints and contact markers; authored middle-foot stance/swing trajectory and baked three-joint inverse kinematics in all six clips; grounded idle/recoil contacts; corpse ground correction; two curved mandibular meshes weighted to the original animated jaws; darker standard PBR base-colour factor and roughness with original image, UV and normal map retained. Original four leg joints and source imagery are retained. This is conventional mesh/rig/material editing; no generated image replaces the source artwork.
